<p>The roots of The Independency Co. started with Leapers, originally a community, and today a research focused project exploring and supporting the mental health of freelancers.</p><p>This annual work produces deep insights into the challenges and gaps faced by freelancers in the UK, and informs much of our work across resources and engagement.</p><p>In 2025, we spoke to over 1000 freelancers to ask about their wellbeing in 2025, and discovered <strong>40% of our freelancers felt their mental health declined, and nearly 30% of those recently joining self-employment felt they had little other choice but to become self-employed.</strong></p><p>Whilst the majority of freelancers see their mental health improve, that&#8217;s only if they&#8217;re putting the structure in place to support themselves. Those who don&#8217;t know where to turn, who didn&#8217;t chose to step into freelancing, who don&#8217;t have a support system, see significant declines.</p><p>Our report highlights hidden issues around isolation, confidence, and the growing number of those who didn&#8217;t choose to step into freelancing, but find themselves feeling like they had little other choice. And for new freelancers, a lack of resources in the first few years is causing a real issue.</p><p>But there&#8217;s positive news too - when freelancers are members of communities, when they have a clear idea of their offering, when they actively work with others, most freelancers say that working this way improves their wellbeing.</p><p>Over the course of 2026, we&#8217;ll be using this research to continue to write and publish resources for freelancers, based upon these insights to provide tangible support and advice for how to navigate challenges in self-employment; and working closely with partners and policymakers to try and make systemic change in to the way the self-employed are supported.</p><div><hr></div><p><strong>The full report is available here: <a href="https://leapers.co/research/2025/report">https://leapers.co/research/2025/report</a></strong></p><div><hr></div><p>With huge thanks to <strong><a href="https://withjack.co.uk/">With Jack</a></strong> for supporting this year&#8217;s research, without whose support, the work would not have been possible.</p><p>And of course, to everyone who shared their insights and stories - thank you for taking part. This work really matters, and has such a positive impact towards making things better for all of us.</p><div><hr></div><p>We&#8217;ll be following up with a series of posts on what the research means and implications for freelancers, hirers, platforms, government and community owners. Stay tuned.</p>
