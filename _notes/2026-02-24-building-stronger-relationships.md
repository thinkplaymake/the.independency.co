<p><a href="https://www.leapers.co/research/">Our research into the issues that freelancers face</a>, consistently show some common themes around financial uncertainty, issues of confidence and isolation. </p><p>However, many of the negative influences on wellbeing - over 50%, come directly from working with clients.</p><p>Issues like late payments, ghosting and poor communication have a very direct impact on the mental health of freelancers.</p><p>We can&#8217;t look at the issue of mental health of freelancers in isolation of how they work with clients - despite being independent, almost all work has an element of working with a customer, client or partner - and the relationship matters.</p><p>Much of our work in the past has been focused on supporting individuals who are working for themselves, but if the river is poisoned upstream, there&#8217;s little point in dealing with the symptoms. Tackling the cause is also important. I often describe mental health as a symptom - of the things you&#8217;re dealing with in life. So if you want to improve your mental health, you need to look at the things which impact it.</p><p>So, in 2026, Leapers will be very much additionally looking towards working with those organisations who hire freelancers - to make a marked improvement in how companies hire, support and work well with freelancers.</p><p>A. BEST PRACTICE: We&#8217;ll be looking at those who are already doing it well - curating examples and best practices, so that others can learn from what is already working to demonstrate the ROI of working effectively with their freelancers. There&#8217;s a strong business case for building stronger relationships with freelancers.</p><p>B. POLICY FRAMEWORK: We&#8217;ll be working with those who want to publish a Freelance Friendly policy statement - our 10 point framework for making it more transparent what a business is already doing, and a roadmap for making improvements each year.</p><p>C. SIGNPOSTING TO SUPPORT: We&#8217;ll be plugging signposting and support into organisations who want to, at the very least, ensure there are useful resources and links to support from third parties for their freelancers.</p><p></p><p>All of the work is based upon our research, and a &#8220;stepped approach&#8221; which hirers can adopt:</p><ol><li><p><strong>Signpost to Support</strong> - the bear minimum, to ensure freelancers know where they can find support when they need it.</p></li><li><p><strong>Do no harm</strong> - removing behaviours which have a tangible negative impact on freelancers, such as late payments</p></li><li><p><strong>Mindset shifts</strong> - moving to a model where freelancers are an integral and supported part of an organisation&#8217;s workforce</p></li></ol><p>None of this means we&#8217;ll be stepping away from supporting freelancers - ultimately, this is a way to both distribute the work we&#8217;re doing to a wider audience, and shift internal behaviours from hirers, to mean we&#8217;re less needed as a project.</p><p></p><h4>How can you help?</h4><p><strong>If you&#8217;re a freelancer</strong> - nominate businesses who you think are already doing it well. If you&#8217;ve worked with an organisation who treated you with respect, got the basics right like paid you on time, or went above and beyond to support and work well with you - introduce us to them. We&#8217;ll include them in our work.</p><p><strong>If you&#8217;re a hirer</strong> - get in touch, and we&#8217;ll walk you through the stepped model. There&#8217;s zero cost to get started.</p>
