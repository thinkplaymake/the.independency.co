<p>Yesterday's IPA data on the agency workforce reported almost 15% decline in headcounts. But does that mean the workforce is shrinking?<br><br>What the data doesn't show is where that talent is going. Our data from the end of last year saw an uptick in the number of people stepping into self-employment (not always by choice).<br><br>There's an explosion in people creating new businesses, consulting, freelancing, along with more and more new models, microstudios, collectives, indy agencies, and all sorts of different shapes of things.<br><br>So, is this a decline or a reshaping and redistribution of the workforce?<br><br>I'd be interested in talking with folks in industry bodies to see how we can better represent the total workforce in data like this, not just employees. <br><br>Our ability to get an accurate picture of our people is declining, if we're not including the diversity of ways of working in our industry.</p>
