Why strategic coaching?

Having someone to work through challenges together is incredibly important. Having someone who understands how independent work works, matters even more.

That's why I work 1:1 with people looking for an outside perspective, from someone on the inside.

I regularly work with

  • Strategy professionals, making sense of the industry and their future career
  • Creative industry professionals looking to take the step into self-employment
  • Established independents looking towards the next chapter of their offering

A little about me

I've worked independently for 30+ years, and ten plus years as a strategist. I've led teams, built and sold businesses, and spent the last decade supporting fellow freelancers and self-starters.

I've worked with brands like LEGO, Google, Accenture, adidas, Innocent, NABS and the Film & TV Charity. And I run a number of community projects focused on independent work, such as Leapers and Outside Perspective, I've supported over 250,000 freelancers through this work.

Most of my work is given away for free via resources, calls, webinars, workshops and community - but sometimes, a 1:1 format is a better fit for specific challenges and questions.
