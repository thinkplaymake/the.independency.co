YunoJuno

Freelancers at pace, within your governance.

Creative and marketing teams in large organisations often need freelancers fast, but have to work inside governance designed for the wider business. That usually means strict IR35 rules, mandated enterprise software for engaging temporary labour, a contingent workforce programme to work within, payment terms that are unfair on freelancers, and long timelines to engage talent, even people the organisation already knows and trusts.

YunoJuno is a freelance management platform built to close that gap. We help in-house teams engage the freelancers they need, quickly, while procurement, HR, legal and finance keep the controls they require.

IR35 handled: status determinations built into the booking process, with clear, documented decisions.
Works with your systems: integrates with enterprise platforms such as SAP Fieldglass and Beeline and operates within MSP-led contingent workforce programmes, keeping freelance spend visible.
Fair pay for freelancers: freelancers are paid promptly by YunoJuno, rather than waiting on long corporate payment terms.
Known talent in under an hour: onboard the freelancers you already work with, with compliance checks such as Right to Work handled for you.
New talent when you need it: access to 150,000+ vetted freelancers across creative and marketing disciplines.
