Privacy Notice

How we use your email address

When you enter your email address:

It is used solely to validate your submission.

Once validated, your email address is permanently deleted.

We do not retain the original email address.

We do not add you to any mailing lists.

We do not sell, share, or publish your email address.

After validation, a hashed (irreversible) version of your email may be stored in a separate system for record integrity purposes. This hashed value cannot be used to identify you or contact you.

We do not keep backups containing your email address.

Lawful basis

We process your email address under legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) for the sole purpose of validating submissions and maintaining system integrity.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to request access, rectification, restriction, or erasure of personal data where applicable. As we do not retain your email address after validation, we may not be able to identify you from the hashed record.

About The Independency Co.

The Independency Co. is a UK organisation dedicated to making freelancing work better. Founded by Matthew Knight and operating as a trading name of Foxlark Strategy Limited, it works across freelancer support, community infrastructure, policy advocacy, and hirer education.

Its projects include Leapers — an award-winning mental health initiative that has supported over 250,000 freelancers — and freelancing.support, a free daily-updated resource for UK independent workers. Outside Perspective is a curated peer community for independent strategists. Manual of Me is a team working tool used by organisations to improve onboarding, remote collaboration, and freelancer relationships.

Matthew Knight chairs the Independency Community Coalition, a collective of 12 UK freelancing communities with over 16,500 members. He is a member of Mind UK's Mental Health at Work Leadership Council and a contributor to parliamentary working groups on independent work and freelancer policy. He has been cited in The Guardian, Bloomberg, the BBC, and Freelancing Magazine, among others.

The Independency Co. is based in Twickenham and serves freelancers, hirers, and community leaders across the UK.
